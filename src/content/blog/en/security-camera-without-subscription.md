People looking for cameras without monthly fees often ask where footage is stored and whether viewing it depends on a subscription. For example, a [privacy community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/privacy/comments/1sy3yr9/tired_of_paying_for_security_camera_subs_any/) asks for local storage without recurring fees. The right setup depends on whether you need a dedicated outdoor camera, remote access or a local recorder.

## Start with the features you need

Ask whether recording continues without a subscription, where files are stored, how you retrieve them, and whether remote viewing or notifications require an account. Check the camera’s own specifications: local storage alone does not tell you whether every feature works offline.

## Using a Mac you already own

Owl Cam records locally using a camera recognized by macOS. One camera, detection, recording and playback remain free after the 14-day Pro trial. Pro is available for $29.99/year or $3.99/month and adds features including multiple cameras and backup. You need a powered-on Mac, an available camera and disk space.

## When another setup is a better fit

Owl Cam currently has no phone app, remote stream or push alerts, and does not directly connect to RTSP / ONVIF cameras. If you need outdoor weatherproof hardware or immediate phone notifications, check alternatives built for those needs. For local Mac recording, test your camera and [read the FAQ](/faq) before deciding.
