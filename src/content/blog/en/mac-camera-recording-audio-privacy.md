Does turning off recorded sound also disable a camera's microphone? A [camera-owner discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/homesecurity/comments/1r88ku1/how_safe_is_it_to_have_private_conversations_near/) asks that question about a different product. It shows a practical privacy concern, not a measured trend. Settings can mean different things across products, so check both the app and the operating system.

## Camera permission and microphone permission are separate

Owl Cam can record microphone audio or turn audio recording off in its recording settings. Camera permission does not itself grant microphone permission. To review which apps may use the microphone, open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Apple explains how to [control microphone access](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchla1b1e1fe/mac). Revoking access prevents Owl Cam from using the microphone until you allow it again; other applications have their own permissions.

## Test the file, not only the switch

1. Choose the audio setting you want in Owl Cam, then record a short test event in a suitable place.
2. End the event and open the saved MP4 in QuickTime. Play it with the volume audible and check whether the result matches your choice.
3. Repeat after changing cameras or audio settings. A silent clip does not by itself prove that every app on the Mac lacks microphone access; check macOS permissions separately.

Owl Cam has no remote live stream or phone app. Video detection, recording and playback run on the Mac. A cloud-synced recording folder or your chosen backup destination can transfer files according to its settings. Existing recordings and copies can retain audio after settings change.

## Agree on the setup in shared spaces

Consider who may appear or be heard, where the camera points, how long files are kept and who can access the Mac or backup folder. Discuss the setup with others in the space and check applicable local rules before recording. See the [FAQ](/faq) and [privacy policy](/privacy) for product details.
