## Choose the right setup for your goal

Owl Cam detects motion and records events on the Mac for later review. It does not provide remote live viewing, a phone app or push alerts. If you need to check on a pet immediately from another location, choose a solution that provides remote viewing. Owl Cam is not a substitute for direct care.

## Set up and test the camera

1. Connect a camera recognized by macOS, select it in Owl Cam, and choose a writable recording folder with free space.
2. Position it securely with the area your pet uses in view. Avoid placing cables or equipment where the animal can reach them.
3. Test with the lighting and camera position you expect to use. Review the resulting clips and adjust the view and detection settings if needed.
4. Keep the Mac awake and the camera connected. The display can sleep; see the [sleep guide](/blog/mac-camera-recording-sleep-lid).

Movement may trigger a recording, but recognition of a pet or capture of every movement is not guaranteed. An animal sitting still may not keep an event recording active. Lighting, distance, obstructions and settings all affect results. Owl Cam's person detection is designed around people; do not assume it identifies pets.

## Review and keep recordings

Use the calendar and timeline on the Mac to find clips. Recordings are MP4 files saved in the folder you choose. A cloud-synced folder may upload them according to that service's settings. Pro can back up finished files, while one camera remains free after the trial. The [FAQ](/faq) explains camera compatibility, storage and other limits.
